## billing-read

[Read credits and usage](/reference/billing-read). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `billing:read` | `workflow-approvals`, `accounts`, `catalog`, `wallet`, `usage`, `activity`, `limits`, `alerts`, `grants`, `deliveries`, `commercial`, `postpaid`, `members`, `preference` |

## billing-write

[Manage billing and spending limits](/reference/billing-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `billing:write` | `ensure-account`, `save-limit`, `read-alert`, `reconcile`, `retry-delivery`, `checkout`, `portal`, `finance-link`, `write-grant`, `revoke-grant`, `request-import`, `configure-postpaid`, `reconcile-postpaid` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
