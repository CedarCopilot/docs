## board

[Manage Cedar boards](/reference/board). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| Cedar app only | `list_boards`, `list_templates`, `read`, `create_board`, `get_schema`, `set_schema`, `add_fields`, `set_view`, `set_board_meta`, `add_card`, `set`, `move`, `comment`, `cards`, `archive`, `list_triggers` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
