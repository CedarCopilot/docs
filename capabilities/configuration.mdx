## config-read

[Read Cedar configuration](/reference/config-read). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `docs:read` | `read-playbook` |
| `crm:read` | `read-settings`, `list-fields`, `list-org-members`, `get-user-identity`, `find-bot-slack-channels` |

## config-write

[Update Cedar configuration](/reference/config-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `playbook:write` | `reassign-aop`, `create-field`, `update-field`, `delete-field`, `link-mapping`, `unlink-mapping`, `add-field-option`, `update-field-option`, `remove-field-option`, `set-stage-criteria` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
