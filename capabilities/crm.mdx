## conversation-read

[Read CRM conversations](/reference/conversation-read). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `crm:read` | `get`, `find`, `search-semantic`, `search-external-crm-deals`, `timeline`, `field-evolution`, `value-as-of`, `describe-field`, `raw-content`, `search` |

## conversation-write

[Update CRM conversations](/reference/conversation-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `crm:read` | `create`, `update-fields`, `create-event`, `mutate`, `reassign-ownership`, `link-external-crm-deal`, `convert-hubspot-lead`, `create-opportunity`, `handoff-crm-record`, `log-email-activity` |
| `email:read` | `sync-gmail-thread` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
