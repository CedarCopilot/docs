## crm-write

| Action | Purpose | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `publish-field-definition` | Publish a versioned account or contact field. | `crm:manage` + `crm:write` |
| `publish-event-definition` | Publish a versioned organization event contract. | `crm:manage` + `crm:write` |
| `ensure-record` | Find or create the workspace account or contact. | `crm:manage` + `crm:write` |
| `update-fields` | Update deal fields or an agent-maintained account or contact list with evidence. | `crm:write` |
| `create-event` | Log deal activity or publish an organization event with named links to Cedar records. | `crm:write` |

See the [parameters, examples, and receipts](/reference/crm-write).

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
