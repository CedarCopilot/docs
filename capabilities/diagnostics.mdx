## debug

[Diagnose & debug a Cedar account](/reference/debug). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `crm:read` | `diagnose-conversation`, `recent-executions`, `execution-detail`, `tool-call-detail`, `health-report`, `test-run` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
