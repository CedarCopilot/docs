| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `docs:read` | `read`, `list`, `search` |

## document-write

[Create, edit, delete, restore, move, or share a Cedar doc](/reference/document-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `docs:read` | `write`, `restore`, `move`, `share`, `request-file` |
| `playbook:write` | `delete` |

Writing to a playbook or daily agenda can require `playbook:write` depending on its path.

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
