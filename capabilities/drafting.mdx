## draft-comms

[Draft or show an email, Slack, or LinkedIn message](/reference/draft-comms). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `email:draft` | `draft-email` |
| `crm:read` | `draft-slack-message` |
| `linkedin:read` | `draft-linkedin-message` |
| `email:read` | `show-draft` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
