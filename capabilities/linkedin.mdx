## linkedin-read

[Read LinkedIn connections, posts, reactions and chats](/reference/linkedin-read). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `linkedin:read` | `list-seats`, `list-connections`, `list-connections-page`, `list-own-posts`, `list-post-reactions`, `profile-viewers`, `post-engagement`, `new-connections`, `list-chats`, `read-chat`, `find-chat`, `search`, `search-parameters`, `connections-page`, `post-page`, `profiles`, `companies`, `screen-prospects`, `outreach-status` |

## linkedin-write

[Send LinkedIn messages or visit profiles — ask permission](/reference/linkedin-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `linkedin:write` | `view-profile`, `send-message`, `send-invitation`, `send-inmail` |
| `linkedin:read` | `sync-chat` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
