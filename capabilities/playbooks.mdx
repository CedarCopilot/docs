## aop-write

[Create or update an AOP/playbook](/reference/aop-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `playbook:write` | `create-aop`, `create-internal`, `update-selection-procedure`, `update-custom-assignment-instruction` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
