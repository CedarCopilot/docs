## load-brain-skills

Availability: **feature-gated**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/load-brain-skills).

Load a Cedar deal-intelligence "brain" skill's full instructions by name. These are the reasoning/analysis skills for deep pipeline questions — how deals are won/lost, cross-deal patterns, rep coaching from real calls, objection/competitor analysis. When your task is such an analysis, you MUST call this tool for the matching skill before proceeding. The one-liners below are only for picking WHICH skill applies — they do not replace loading it. ## Access and results

Use your authenticated connection's `tools/list` to discover what is available. Role, feature, target, and resource checks apply in addition to the baseline scopes. Inspect the returned operation result before reporting success; a queued or proposed action is not a completed external action. See [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and [calling tools](/getting-started/calling-tools).
