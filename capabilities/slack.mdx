## slack-read

[Read live Slack](/reference/slack-read). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `slack:read` | `search-live`, `read-thread`, `read-file` |

## slack-write

[Manage Slack channels and sync threads](/reference/slack-write). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `slack:read` | `sync-thread` |
| `slack:write` | `create-channel`, `invite-members`, `invite-external` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
