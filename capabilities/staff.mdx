## analytics

Availability: **staff-only**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/analytics).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

INTERNAL/STAFF-TIER ONLY — rejects any caller that is not Cedar staff (see this tool's top-of-file comment for why the check lives here and not only on the wrapped tRPC procedures). Org-wide draft-analytics and growth reporting. ACTIONS: (1) attribution-rate — of a user's (or all users') actual outbound emails, what fraction originated from a Cedar draft. Optionally scoped to one userId and/or one aopName. (2) attribution-by-category — draft volume/send-rate/avg-similarity broken down by email category, for one AOP (defaults to "Deals"). (3) similarity-distribution — histogram of word-count-similarity scores across sent drafts, for one AOP (defaults to "Deals"). (4) user-tiers — segments all active users into cold/low/high attribution tiers for one AOP (defaults to "Deals"). (5) overview — per-user draft totals/send-rate for ALL users at once; startDate/endDate/aopName are all optional (defaults: startDate 5 days ago, endDate unbounded). (6) monthly-growth — North Star email-attribution rate (with numerator/denominator broken out) plus PostHog engagement (DAU/WAU/MAU/stickiness) and per-user attribution spread, hardcoded to the "Deals" AOP. Its attribution count has a documented dedup imprecision (thread + 5-minute bucket, not message_id) — see this action's schema description. Each action takes its params in a same-named object (e.g. action: "attribution-rate" pairs with an attributionRate: &#123;...&#125; object) — omit every params object except the one matching your chosen action.

</Accordion>

<Note>Cedar staff only. Customer-facing pipeline metrics use conversation-analytics.</Note>

### Supported operations

| Operation | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attribution-rate` | Measure what fraction of actual outbound email originated from Cedar drafts. | `internal` |
| `attribution-by-category` | Break down draft volume, send rate and similarity by email category. | `internal` |
| `similarity-distribution` | Inspect the distribution of drafted-versus-sent word-count similarity. | `internal` |
| `user-tiers` | Segment active users by email-attribution levels for the selected AOP. | `internal` |
| `overview` | Read per-user draft totals and send rates across the supported user population. | `internal` |
| `monthly-growth` | Read staff growth metrics, attribution and engagement, with the documented attribution deduplication limits. | `internal` |

## billing-admin

Availability: **staff-only**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/billing-admin).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Cedar staff only. organization-accounts paginates member organizations; provision-organization-accounts requires organizationIds/reviewReference and creates no funds or grants. organization-monthly-limits previews or applies (apply:true) $50/$80/$100 then each $100 notifications, preserving customer limits. Preview canonical Stripe subscriptions before verify-import with reviewed snapshotHash/ownershipReference. Immutable offers, catalog and prospective allowances require approved terms; never invent prices or modify existing Stripe plans/quantities. payment-events reads outcomes; retry-payment requires reviewReference/idempotencyKey and revalidates evidence. postpaid reads organization configuration. configure-postpaid requires postpaid:&#123;accountId,enabled,expectedVersion,subscriptionId?,rolloutReference&#125;; only the real staff operator may perform this rollout. It records staff authority and customerConsentAsserted:false, never customer opt-in or impersonation. Existing payer/eligibility checks remain. process-postpaid processes frozen usage or a known batchId; no caller-supplied amounts. correct-usage requires a reviewed correction.

</Accordion>

<Note>Cedar staff only. A billing scope alone does not authorize administration.</Note>

## cedar-staff-debug

Availability: **staff-only**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/cedar-staff-debug).

Cedar staff only. Start or end a debug session that lets you see Cedar exactly as a specific user does, for observability and troubleshooting. While active, tools read and act on that user's account so you can diagnose their setup, executions, and data — and nothing is saved to their history. Call with `email` or `userId` to start; call with `clear:true` to end. It auto-ends after 30 min of inactivity, and every reply reminds you whose account you are debugging.

Single-call family. Baseline scope: `internal`.
