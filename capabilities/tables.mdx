## table

[Manage Cedar tables](/reference/table). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `docs:read` | `read`, `create`, `add_columns`, `add_rows`, `set`, `clear`, `delete_rows`, `import`, `fill`, `bindings`, `update_columns`, `remove_columns`, `reorder_columns`, `update_view`, `move_row`, `insert_section`, `export` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
