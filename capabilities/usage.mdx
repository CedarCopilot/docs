## usage-observations-read

Availability: **external**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/usage-observations-read).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Inspect configured usage producers or your own durable shadow measurements for an authorized billing account. Shows known/unknown quantity and delivery status without enabling AI charges, submitting usage, or invoking providers. Account finance users still see only their own observation summaries.

</Accordion>

### Supported operations

| Operation | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `producers` | Discover configured producers of durable usage observations. | `billing:read` |
| `list` | Read the caller's shadow measurements, quantity certainty and ledger-delivery status. | `billing:read` |

## usage-observations-retry

Availability: **external**. [Parameters and responses](/reference/usage-observations-retry).

Retry delivery of your existing blocked shadow measurement to the usage ledger. Requires current spending permission and the original grant and shadow meter version. Reuses the immutable saved receipt; cannot change amounts, create grants, charge credits, or repeat provider work.

Single-call family. Baseline scope: `billing:write`.
