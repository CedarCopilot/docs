## ui-config

[Configure Cedar UI canvases](/reference/ui-config). Operations are grouped by baseline access.

| Baseline access | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `crm:read` | `list-canvases`, `create-canvas`, `update-canvas`, `delete-canvas` |
| `playbook:write` | `configure-overview` |

For a specific request, check [permissions](/getting-started/permissions) and the schema returned by your connection. Account, resource and action checks still apply.
