---

Cedar exposes iMessage as a configured user-notification destination through `notify send`. The notification contract supports the tapback choices `thumbsup`, `thumbsdown`, `heart`, `haha`, `exclamation` and `question`.

Use the user's configured notification preference or explicitly choose the intended supported channel. Inspect the returned delivery result before reporting that the message arrived. Changing a preference does not prove a device or channel is connected and deliverable.

This capability does not establish a general iMessage inbox, arbitrary contact search, bulk outreach or scheduled-iMessage API. The current `schedule-send` channels are email, LinkedIn and Slack.
