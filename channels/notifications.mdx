`notify` sends a proactive message to the user through the selected supported channel. It can deliver Slack, iMessage, email or WhatsApp notifications where configured. Leaving the channel unset uses the user's setting; an explicit channel selects that destination for the message.

## Slack destinations and interactions

Use `list-slack-channels` to discover accessible channel IDs and the user's DM before naming a destination. Slack notifications can include structured action/link buttons, reactions and thread replies. iMessage supports the tapback choices exposed in the schema.

These are notifications, not a blanket customer messaging API. A WhatsApp notification capability does not imply a general WhatsApp inbox or arbitrary recipient-send family. Use the channel-specific guides to distinguish notification delivery from live conversation operations.

## Alerts and digests

Use alerts for meaningful changes and a scheduled digest for repeated low-urgency updates. Put relevance filters before notification creation, retain processed source identifiers and choose the intended destination. The queue does not automatically collapse many notifications into one digest.

Inspect delivery results. If a notification fails, do not route it through a customer draft/send path as an implicit fallback. An in-chat response is also separate from proactive notification delivery.
