Cedar brings communication context and channel actions together. Start with the channel you want to use, then use shared drafting, scheduling or notification tools where your workflow spans channels.

## Communication channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="LinkedIn" icon="linkedin" href="/linkedin/overview">Profiles, prospecting, engagement, conversations, invitations, sending, limits and automatic queueing.</Card>
<Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="/modules/email">Search, attachments, drafts, reviewed delivery and classified open/click evidence.</Card>
<Card title="Slack" icon="slack" href="/slack/overview">Search, read and sync threads, watch selected channels, and deliver useful alerts.</Card>
<Card title="WhatsApp" icon="comment" href="/integrations/whatsapp">Connection consent, chat synchronization, deal linking and user notifications.</Card>
<Card title="iMessage" icon="message" href="/channels/imessage">Configured user notifications and supported tapback behavior.</Card>
<Card title="Meetings and calendars" icon="calendar" href="/channels/meetings">Meeting evidence and recorder sync, with separate calendar event and availability tools.</Card>
</CardGroup>

LinkedIn's [sending and messaging](/linkedin/messaging), [limits and recommendations](/linkedin/limits), and [automatic queueing](/automation/queueing) live together in the LinkedIn section. Email tracking sits inside Email. The [Calendar family](/capabilities/calendar) covers event creation, changes and availability independently of meeting-recording sources.

## Shared channel tools

| Tool family | What it provides | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `draft-comms` | Channel-specific preparation for email, Slack and LinkedIn | [Drafting](/channels/drafting) |
| `schedule-send` | Approved future delivery for email, Slack and LinkedIn | [Scheduling](/channels/scheduling) |
| `notify` | Supported user-facing alert delivery | [Notifications](/channels/notifications) |

Channel tools operate on connected accounts and their permissions. Persistent channel monitoring is configured through [agent-platform playbooks and triggers](/automation/playbooks). Captured context becomes [Brain evidence](/brain/evidence-and-records), and [Pipeline intelligence](/intelligence/overview) uses it to explain deal activity and outcomes.
