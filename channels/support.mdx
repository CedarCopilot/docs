---

`send-user-feedback` saves a problem or question for the Cedar team and attempts a Slack notification. Provide a concise description of what was tried, what happened and the expected result. Include relevant run, conversation and agent IDs when available.

The response distinguishes saving the report from delivering the team notification. If `notificationDelivered` is false, the report was saved but its Slack alert failed; do not report that both steps succeeded.

Keep credentials, tokens and private email bodies out of the report. Exact errors and identifiers usually provide the investigation context. Load `customer-support` for the account-aware support workflow and use [diagnostics](/modules/diagnostics) to inspect an execution before claiming a root cause.
