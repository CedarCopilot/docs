The usage families expose durable shadow measurements associated with an authorized billing account.

| Operation | Capability |
| --- | --- |
| `usage-observations-read producers` | Discover configured usage producers |
| `usage-observations-read list` | Inspect the caller's measurements, known/unknown quantity and delivery state with supported filters |
| `usage-observations-retry` | Retry eligible delivery of a saved blocked measurement to the usage ledger |

Reading observations does not turn on AI charging, submit fresh usage or call a provider. Finance access does not expand the observation summaries to every user's underlying activity.

A retry uses the immutable saved receipt and requires current spending permission, the original grant and compatible shadow-meter version. It cannot change the amount, create a grant, charge credits or repeat provider work. Distinguish a retried ledger-delivery record from re-running the user's original operation.
