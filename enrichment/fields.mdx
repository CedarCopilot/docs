The web enrichment builder uses **112 normalized field names**. The catalog below is generated from that exact vocabulary and the registered enrichment operations. A selectable field without a registered operation is not currently fillable by a provider lookup through that catalog; it may still be supplied by input, lookup, AI or other supported column sources. Mapped coverage also does not guarantee a provider record contains a value.

The external research discovery catalog is narrower and is documented in [Enrichment and research tools](/capabilities/research). This page records the web builder's field vocabulary and coverage; it does not add external MCP actions.
