---
title: "Review deals in Cedar"
description: "Use a deal you already know to check its evidence and choose the next step."
---

You need access to a Cedar workspace with at least one synced deal. Pick an account you recognize so you can tell whether you have found the right record.

1. Open **Pipeline** in Cedar. Choose a pipeline view you can access. A list view is useful for finding a known account. A Kanban view groups records into columns.
2. Open the matching deal. Check its company and participants, then inspect a recent email or meeting associated with it. If the account has several opportunities, use the participants and source details to distinguish them.
3. Review the open work associated with the deal. For example, after a discovery call, look for the rep who promised pricing and the date they committed to. Compare the task with the original message before changing its owner or deadline.
4. Decide whether there is already a next step. Keep the existing task when it covers the commitment. If a follow-up is missing, use the [task lifecycle guide](/workspace/tasks) to create it with an owner and due date.

You are finished when you can identify the evidence for the next action and the person responsible for it. If the deal is absent, check the selected view and filters, then confirm your account can access the underlying record. [Record visibility](/brain/visibility) explains what a partial result means.

For a proposed new CRM opportunity, open **Review deal** on the proposal. **Open in chat** carries that proposal into the review conversation. Inspect its evidence and proposed CRM values before approving any write. Opening the review itself leaves that decision pending.
