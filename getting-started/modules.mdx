## Complete inventories

The contract contains **44 external registrations** (including staff and feature-gated entries) and **8 app-only tools**. The [capability index](/reference/coverage) covers every schema selector; your session's `tools/list` determines actual access.

The platform catalog includes **46 library templates**, **13 system-agent definitions** and **12 playbook trigger types**. The [skill catalog](/skills/index) includes **20 skills** and **48 named resources**. These are distinct inventories, not additive counts of APIs.
