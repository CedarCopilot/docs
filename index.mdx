## Follow a discovery-call task through the layers

When a workflow crosses systems, decide which work the agent can finish and where the rep’s judgment changes the next step. See [Design a human-in-the-loop workflow](/getting-started/human-in-the-loop) to see how a rep chooses whom to approach from a deal org chart and then approves the outreach sequences.

| Layer | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | The meeting transcript records a commitment. The sales playbook says which commitments need follow-up. |
| Agent superpowers | The agent reads the deal, checks the transcript and prepares a source-backed follow-up. |
| Agent execution | [Cedar’s harness](/agent-platform/execution) starts on a configured trigger, or [your runtime](/agent-platform/your-runtime) starts on your schedule. |
| Agent outputs | The run saves a brief with the proposed next step and a link to its evidence. |
| Where outputs go | A follow-up task carries the owner and due date. Its [Task View](/tasks/task-views) gives the rep a queue of work. |
| Where reps work | The rep opens the task in Cedar, reviews a configured Slack card, or [asks their assistant](/workspace/review-with-mcp) to retrieve the brief. |

## Examples

| Workflow | Context and strategy | Agent work | Rep experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account prioritization | Usage, engagement, conversations and your account-scoring rules | Maintain evidence-backed account research and priorities | Account table or brief plus owned follow-up tasks in a Task View |
| Slack signal monitoring | Authorized channel activity and your buying-signal/friction definitions | Inspect relevant messages and prepare a supported next step | A task with linked evidence, reviewed from Cedar or Slack |
| Pipeline revival | Deal history, relevant case studies and product updates | Draft a specific, useful reason to reconnect | Review the draft, send deliberately and track the resulting task state |

These are workflow designs, not a promise that a template connects every source or activates every trigger automatically. Start by configuring and testing one workflow and its rep-facing destination.
