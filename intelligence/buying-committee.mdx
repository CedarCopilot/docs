---
title: "Map the buying committee"
description: "Build an evidence-backed picture of stakeholders and gaps on a deal."
---

Start with the participants in deal emails and meetings. Record each person’s buying role and the evidence for it. A security lead reviewing a purchase can belong on the chart even when their reporting line is unknown.

On an enabled deal, use the [graph tool](/reference/graph) to inspect existing nodes and connections before updating the chart. Add a reporting edge only when the evidence supports that relationship. Keep a prospective contact distinct from someone the rep has already met. Flag missing roles or unverified relationships for the rep’s next conversation.

The [org mapping template](/agent-platform/library#org-map) describes the agent workflow. Open the resulting [deal org chart](/agent-platform/graphs) to review the relationships.
