---
title: "Analyze customer & competitive themes"
description: "Identify recurring objections, competitor mentions and product feedback across deals."
---

Choose a specific question and cohort, such as “Which objections recur in deals that stalled after discovery?” Use the [analysis decomposition skill](/skills/analysis-decomposition) to define the evidence you need. Inspect the original conversations behind each candidate theme and count distinct deals so repeated mentions in one thread do not inflate the result.

Record the pattern, its supporting sources and a counterexample when available. Keep hypotheses separate from reasons buyers explicitly gave. Use the [objection library](/agent-platform/library#objection-library) or [competitor intelligence](/agent-platform/library#competitor-intel) template for a repeatable review. To retrieve an already approved answer for a rep, use the [knowledge base](/knowledge/overview).
