---
title: "Qualify & score leads"
description: "Evaluate a lead against your team’s qualification criteria."
---

Start with the [lead scoring template](/agent-platform/library#lead-scoring) and your team’s definition of a qualified lead. For an inbound request, identify the company and role, then compare the available evidence with each criterion. Record where the evidence is missing so a rep can decide what to ask next.

Use [account research](/modules/research) to fill gaps and the [field-query skill](/skills/field-query) to inspect the deal fields behind the assessment. Keep the score, the reason for it and the suggested next step together. A CRM change such as converting a lead uses the [CRM approval workflow](/crm/records-and-repair).
