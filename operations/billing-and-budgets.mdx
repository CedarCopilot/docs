---
title: "Spending accounts, limits and delivery recovery"
description: "Inspect every billing workflow and distinguish financial state from provider work."
---

Billing tools use authorized accounts, exact microcredit strings and versioned policy. One credit is 1,000,000 microcredits. Keep these units distinct from provider-native credits and currency.

## Read capabilities

`billing-read` covers `accounts`, `catalog`, `wallet`, `usage`, `activity`, `limits`, `alerts`, `grants`, `deliveries`, `commercial`, `postpaid`, `members`, `preference` and `workflow-approvals`. Start by discovering authorized accounts. Pending workflow spending approvals are completed by the user through Cedar Billing.

## Write capabilities

| Action group | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| Account and policy | `ensure-account`, `save-limit`, `read-alert` |
| Balance and delivery recovery | `reconcile`, `retry-delivery` |
| Hosted finance workflows | `checkout`, `portal`, `finance-link` |
| Spending grants | `write-grant`, `revoke-grant` |
| Imports and monthly billing | `request-import`, `configure-postpaid`, `reconcile-postpaid` |

An unfunded account does not contain purchased credits. A checkout link does not prove payment. Credits follow verified payment processing. A grant does not override account/member/feature/meter limits.

## Observations and retry boundaries

`usage-observations-read producers/list` reads durable measurements and delivery state. `usage-observations-retry` retries delivery of an existing immutable measurement; it does not rerun the paid provider request or edit its quantity.

Inspect an operation's exact recovery contract before retrying. Reconciliation, payment processing, ledger delivery and enrichment are different operations with different duplicate risks.

## Staff administration

`billing-admin` adds catalog/offers/allowances, payment events/retries, subscription reconciliation, import verification, usage corrections and organization account/monthly-limit administration. These are explicitly staff-only capabilities; customer integrations should use their authorized read/write surface.
