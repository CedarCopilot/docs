---
title: "Research an account before outreach"
description: "Prepare a short account brief from deal history and public evidence, with a clear reason to contact someone or hold off."
---

Suppose a rep sees a company hiring its first RevOps lead. Before approaching the hiring manager, the rep needs to know whether a teammate already owns the relationship and whether the hiring signal relates to a problem the company has actually described.

## Start with the existing relationship

Open the company or deal in Cedar. Verify the domain and contact identity. Search for other conversations at that company and check recent messages, meetings, active tasks and prior outreach. If a teammate is already managing the conversation, prepare context for that owner.

> Example request for Cedar chat: “Prepare a brief for my next conversation with this account. Find the latest buyer-stated goal, who owns the next step and the original source for each. List anything we still need to ask. Leave outreach as a draft.”

## Fill the specific evidence gap

Use the least additional research that resolves your question.

| Question | Where to look | What to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What has the buyer already told us? | The original message, meeting or other source event in the deal | Verbatim evidence, date and source reference |
| Who is this person or company? | Existing profile, then an authorized enrichment lookup against the verified identifier if necessary | Person or company ID, provider and freshness where available |
| What has the company publicly announced? | The company's own announcement or careers page. Cedar chat can read a known public URL using the website capability when available | Exact URL, relevant passage and publication date if the page supplies one |
| What remains uncertain? | Compare the evidence above with the claim you want to make | A specific open question for the owner or buyer |

A job posting shows what the company is recruiting for. It cannot establish who has purchasing authority. A provider's retrieval time also cannot establish when an announcement was published.

## Save the brief and choose the next step

Save a short document with four parts: the account goal, supporting evidence, unresolved questions, and a recommended next step with an owner. Keep sensitive account context inside the appropriate Cedar audience. Open its source links and verify that each supports the claim beside it. If the evidence warrants contacting someone, prepare a draft for the owner to review. If the case is weak, leave the question with the account instead of manufacturing a reason to reach out.

Most briefs can start with the existing record and known public pages. If you choose a paid Autumn research job inside Cedar, first define the public target and scope and approve the credit cost.
