---
title: "Prepare and review a sequence for one person"
description: "Build a cadence for a known contact, choose a sender, and inspect every step before starting delivery."
---

Use a sequence when one person needs several timed touches. For example, after a buyer asks for a technical brief, you might prepare an email containing the brief, wait two business days in your own planned timeline, and follow up on LinkedIn. Set each delay or send time explicitly; check the resulting dates in review.

## 1. Open the person's sequences

Open the contact in the deal workspace and choose **Sequence**. Check their saved or active sequences before choosing **Create new**. If the contact lacks an identity, add an email address or LinkedIn profile first. When starting from a conversation instead, select the intended person before writing copy.

## 2. Choose the sender and build the steps

Choose the sender for this person's cadence. Add and order the email, LinkedIn connection, LinkedIn message, InMail, Slack or wait steps you need. Set the recipient, timing and message for each delivery step. For a Slack step, choose a destination available through the connected sender's workspace. Review the actual destination closely: delivering the step can post externally visible copy.

Choose **Save draft** to leave the sequence ready for further editing. You can reopen it from the person's sequence list. Active sequences linked to a conversation are also accessible from its task area.

## 3. Review before starting

Choose **Review & start** to save the draft and prepare its delivery review. Inspect each recipient, connected sender, message, order and delivery time in the saved plan. Only approve the plan when you intend Cedar to begin delivery. If someone changed it after your review, reopen the latest revision. A saved draft or an open review does not send a step.

If the review is delivered through a configured Slack task, you can inspect the steps there. Open an editable step to revise delivery and timing, recipients or message copy. Already sent steps remain part of the delivery history. Check the per-step state when resuming a sequence rather than assuming every step has gone out.

## Preparing drafts from an MCP client

With the appropriate grants, the `sequence` family can list and read existing sequences, create or revise a draft, or attach a prepared sequence to an eligible task. An update needs the current sequence version. A task attachment needs the current task and assignment revisions. If either changed, reread and review before retrying. Draft authoring and task attachment provide no delivery approval.
