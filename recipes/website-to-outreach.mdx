---
title: "Turn a website signal into a reviewed follow-up"
description: "Qualify an identified visitor or demo request, prepare one draft, and verify what happened before contacting the buyer."
---

A demo form names someone who asked to hear from you. A visit to your pricing page provides weaker evidence. Preserve that difference in the review record and in any proposed message.

## 1. Receive an identified event

Start with a provisioned Cedar Input for RB2B visitor records, or a registered HTTP playbook webhook for your own form. Configure the matching input or trigger before sending an event. For example, a fictional demo form might include an event ID, the form URL, submission time, the buyer's submitted work email and the question they asked. Keep provider credentials out of review records. [Website inputs and payloads](/signals/website)

## 2. Decide whether to follow up

Resolve the person and company using the identifiers actually supplied. Check existing CRM conversations, open tasks and prior outreach before creating work. If two people could match, leave the row for identity review. If this is already an active deal, direct the event to its owner with the source attached. Enrich only when you need information for a specific qualification decision and the provider use is authorized.

Use a stable source/event key and keep the last person-level decision with the working record. Configure the workflow so a retry or repeat visit updates the evidence and reuses the decision. The webhook itself does not deduplicate your business decisions.

## 3. Prepare the review

Save the source, timestamp, person, company, relationship, reason for follow-up and proposed next action in the task or table row. Draft against the current channel conversation. For a form submission, answer the buyer's stated question. For passive website activity, avoid claiming the person requested outreach. If the owner or recipient is unclear, leave an actionable review item instead of scheduling a send.

## 4. Check the outcome

Try one controlled event with fictional data and an internal review destination. Confirm that one review record appears, links to the right source and carries the expected owner and draft. Replay that event and verify the configured workflow reuses the record. HTTP acceptance confirms receipt; it cannot confirm completion of the playbook.

When the rep wants to contact the buyer, have them review the exact recipient, sender, copy and timing. Use the appropriate [reviewed send](/email/drafts-and-delivery) or [scheduled delivery](/channels/scheduling) flow. Keep a queued item recorded as queued until the channel reports a delivery outcome.
