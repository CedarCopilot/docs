## Capabilities

[Billing overview](/modules/billing) · [Complete capabilities](/capabilities/billing).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Read your organization billing account and any historical personal records, credit balance, usage receipts and durable activity, limits, alerts, spending grants, members, payment offers/subscriptions, monthly postpaid status and invoices, organization payer context or delivery status. List accounts first. Every result uses your authenticated permissions; finance access does not reveal another user’s deal or provider payload. Amounts are exact microcredit strings: 1 credit = 1,000,000 microcredits. workflow-approvals lists pending workflow spending confirmations; only the user can approve in Cedar Billing. This never purchases credits or starts paid work.

</Accordion>

## Operations and scopes

| Action | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `workflow-approvals` | List pending workflow spending confirmations that the user can approve in Cedar Billing. | `billing:read` |
| `accounts` | Discover authorized organization billing accounts and historical personal records. | `billing:read` |
| `catalog` | Read available billing catalog entries and permitted offers. | `billing:read` |
| `wallet` | Inspect exact credit balances and wallet state. | `billing:read` |
| `usage` | Read authorized usage receipts and accounting evidence. | `billing:read` |
| `activity` | Read durable billing-account activity. | `billing:read` |
| `limits` | Inspect account/member/feature/meter limits and their current versions. | `billing:read` |
| `alerts` | Read spending and billing alerts. | `billing:read` |
| `grants` | Inspect spending grants available through the caller's authority. | `billing:read` |
| `deliveries` | Inspect billing-related delivery state. | `billing:read` |
| `commercial` | Read supported payment offers, subscription and commercial context. | `billing:read` |
| `postpaid` | Read monthly postpaid configuration, verified subscription context and invoices. | `billing:read` |
| `members` | Read authorized billing-account members. | `billing:read` |
| `preference` | Read the supported billing preference for the caller. | `billing:read` |

Path, account role, feature gates, target ownership, and billing permissions can impose additional requirements.
