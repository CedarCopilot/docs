## Capabilities

[Data services overview](/channels/data-services) · [Complete capabilities](/capabilities/data-services).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Update your enrichment defaults or your organization’s data policy. Read config-read.read-settings with sections:&#91;"data_services"&#93; first for current versions. Crustdata is the rich-research default; automatic conversation company metadata and pictures stay on Fiber. Crustdata watchers are fixed. Organization writes require an administrator. Null resets an override to inheritance. This changes future defaults only; it does not activate watchers, grant spending, buy credits, or modify existing watches.

</Accordion>

## Operations and scopes

| Action | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `update` | Change versioned enrichment defaults or administrator-controlled organization data policy for future work. | `playbook:write` |

Path, account role, feature gates, target ownership, and billing permissions can impose additional requirements.
