## Capabilities

[Email tracking overview](/email/tracking) · [Complete capabilities](/capabilities/email-tracking).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Read the authenticated sender’s tracked email opens and clicks. list returns paginated message summaries, optionally filtered by Gmail thread or authorized Cedar conversation. get returns one trackingId and classified events. openCount/clickCount count likely human activity; raw counts include image proxies and automation. Unknown or automated requests do not prove an open. recipientEmail is the intended recipient of a single-recipient send, never verified reader identity; shared emails and forwarding cannot identify who read. No IP geolocation or device inference identifies a person. Read-only; never sends or enables tracking.

</Accordion>

## Operations and scopes

| Action | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `list` | List tracked-email records and available engagement evidence with supported filters. | `email:read` |
| `get` | Inspect a selected tracked email's observed open/click and classification details. | `email:read` |

Path, account role, feature gates, target ownership, and billing permissions can impose additional requirements.
