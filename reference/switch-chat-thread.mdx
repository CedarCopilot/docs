## Capabilities

[Chat context and history overview](/agent-platform/chat) · [Complete capabilities](/capabilities/chat).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Switch the user's active chat to one of their own past threads — use when the user wants to actually go back to / resume / open a past conversation, not just be told about it or have you reason over it (that's load-chat-history).  Pass &#123; threadId &#125;. The user's screen navigates to that thread; you do not get its content back from this tool. Call it last in your turn — anything you say after this won't be visible until the user switches back.

</Accordion>

Required baseline scope: `in-app`. Additional role and target checks apply.
