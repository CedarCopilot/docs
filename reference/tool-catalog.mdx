Use this catalog to find tools by product area. Each reference identifies where the tool is available. Your role, scopes and enabled features determine which tools appear in your connection’s `tools/list`.

Action selectors and nested configuration options are indexed in the [capability coverage index](/reference/coverage). Registered skill families and their named resources are listed in the [skill catalog](/skills/catalog). [Download the complete catalog](/reference/catalog.json).

## Surface boundaries

App-only operations are labeled inside their parent area with explicit availability warnings. Enabled accounts can edit their authorized deal org charts and author reviewable sequence drafts. Creating a draft does not enroll a person or start sending. Third-party campaign management remains outside this contract. See [capability boundaries](/getting-started/capability-boundaries).
