## Capabilities

[Usage and delivery recovery overview](/channels/usage) · [Complete capabilities](/capabilities/usage).

<Accordion title="Full tool behavior and constraints">

Inspect configured usage producers or your own durable shadow measurements for an authorized billing account. Shows known/unknown quantity and delivery status without enabling AI charges, submitting usage, or invoking providers. Account finance users still see only their own observation summaries.

</Accordion>

## Operations and scopes

| Action | Capability | Baseline scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `producers` | Discover configured producers of durable usage observations. | `billing:read` |
| `list` | Read the caller's shadow measurements, quantity certainty and ledger-delivery status. | `billing:read` |

Path, account role, feature gates, target ownership, and billing permissions can impose additional requirements.
