Discover, select, and query CRM field data (crm_conversation_field_values) for one or many conversations, then present results as a table when they warrant one. Covers competitor mentions, current provider/vendor, contract or renewal details, close/renewal dates or other deal timing, objections, pain points, and any other extracted or custom field — for a single deal ('did Acme mention X') or the whole pipeline ('which deals mentioned X', 'deals closing this week'). Load this BEFORE run-sql, conversation's find action, or conversation-read ever touch crm_conversation_field_values, even when the question also sounds like a plain listing/filter question or the right field seems obvious — filtering on a value still requires knowing which field_id holds it for this org, and a short question is not evidence the concept lives in only one field.
