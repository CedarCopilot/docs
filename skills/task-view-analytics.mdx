On an enabled account, call `load-skill` with `{"name":"task-view-analytics"}` for read-only analysis. Identify the rep, view and time window the question concerns. Use the view’s supported reads and distinguish observed outcomes from inferred progress. A visible task, a prepared draft, a completed task and a delivered message describe different events.

[Task View workflow](/tasks/task-views) · [Task visibility](/tasks/visibility)

## Answer a rep’s activity question

Confirm the dates and timezone, list accessible views, then narrow by view, rep and outcome. Keep views with zero activity in the comparison. Open an individual task’s chronological evidence before interpreting a change as draft adoption, delivery or a failure.
