---
title: "Conversation Overview"
description: "Review the fields and documents selected for a deal’s playbook."
---

Open a deal in Cedar and go to its overview to see the fields and documents selected for that playbook. After a discovery call, for example, the rep can check the current next step and open a follow-up brief if the playbook includes it.

Use Conversation Overview when the team needs context while reviewing one deal. Use a [Task View](/tasks/task-views) when a rep needs a queue of owned actions across deals. A brief can provide context on the deal while a linked task carries the follow-up deadline.

## Choose what appears

A RevOps admin can use [configure-overview](/reference/ui-config) to select fields and documents. Read the current configuration, preserve the entries the team still uses, and reopen an affected deal to check the result. To change which deals appear in a pipeline list, use [saved view filters](/workspace/saved-views#keep-or-discard-pipeline-filters).
