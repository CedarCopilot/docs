---
title: "Review work with your agent via MCP"
description: "Use your connected assistant to review a follow-up task, its prepared output and the deal evidence."
---

After a discovery call, you may already have a task to follow up. Your assistant can help you review the work in the context of that call. Start with a [connected MCP client](/getting-started/quickstart) signed in to the Cedar account that owns the task.

> Find my open follow-up task from the discovery call. Show me the prepared follow-up and the source for the commitment it mentions. Leave the task open for my review.

1. **Find the right task.** Your assistant can use the [task tool](/reference/task) to list your work. If several tasks match, choose the one with the right deal and meeting date.
2. **Open the saved output.** For an attached brief, read the linked [document](/reference/document-read). For a prepared sequence, use the task’s delivery review to inspect its saved plan and progress. If no output is attached, ask the assistant to show what is available before creating anything new.
3. **Check the commitment.** Compare the proposed follow-up with the [deal evidence](/brain/evidence-and-records). Check who promised what and when. If the source is unavailable to your connection, open the deal in Cedar to review it.
4. **Decide what happens next.** Ask for a revision or review the prepared work in Cedar. Sending a message requires your approval of the recipient, content and timing.

The same underlying task can also appear in a [Task View](/tasks/task-views) or on a [Slack task card](/slack/task-views). To build the automation that prepares the work, see [Run your own agent with Cedar](/agent-platform/your-runtime).
